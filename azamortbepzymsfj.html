capitalism is the opposition of robbery

robbery is bad for the economy

normally phrased as 'the state should not interfere in the economy, in the free market mechanism'

the connection with robbery: the state can interfere only by robbery

'interference' is the motivation of economic actors to act differently than they would organically; motivation can be either negative or positive; both are robbery

negative motivation is punishment, which is a robbery of economic freedom or of wealth

positive motivation is support, the wealth for which comes from taxation, which (in that case) is robbery
