lemma

proposition

(d/dt) y = y^3 * v * a / c^2

proof

(d/dt) y

=

(d/dt) (1 - v^2 / c^2) ^ (-1/2)

=

(-1/2) * (1 - v^2 / c^2) ^ (-3/2) * (-1 / c^2) * 2 * v * (dv/dt)

=

(1 - v^2 / c^2) ^ (-3/2) * v * (dv/dt) / c^2

=

y^3 * v * a / c^2

derivation of force

F

=

in classical mechanics force is defined as F = m * a; relativity breaks this, as it has a speed limit [acceleration must slow down over time]

so we have to use the other definition, via momentum

dp / dt

=

(d/dt) (y * m * v)

=

differentiate product

m * ((d/dt) y * v + y * (d/dt) v)

=

using the lemma about ((d/dt) y)

m * (y^3 * v * a * v / c^2 + y * a)

=

m * a * y * (y^2 * v^2 / c^2 + 1)

=

m * a * y * y ^ 2

=

y ^ 3 * m * a
